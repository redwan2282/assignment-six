FitLog - Workout Library:-


FitLog is a simple app to help you track your workouts. It helps you manage your gym routine and keep track of the exercises you want to do.


About the Project: 


This app is for people who want to organize their gym time. You can see a list of exercises, learn how to do them, add them to your daily plan, and save them for later.


Technologies Used
1.Next.js
2.React
3.Tailwind CSS
4.DaisyUI
5.React Toastify


Key Features:

1.Browse Exercises: See a list of different workouts with details like equipment needed, sets, and reps.
2.Detailed Instructions: Click on any exercise to see step-by-step instructions and learn how to do it correctly.
3.Daily Planner: Add up to 5 exercises to your "Today's Plan" to keep your workout focused.
4.Saved List: Save your favorite exercises in a separate list so you can find them easily later.
5.Workout Stats: Automatically see the total time and total calories burned for your selected workout plan, with options to sort them.
